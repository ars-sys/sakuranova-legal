English

SakuraNova ("the app") is provided by ARS, K.K. ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we do not collect

The app has no account and does not ask for your name, email address or other personal details. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

Information kept only on your device

  • Your settings, the level you chose, your study goals and your word book.
  • Voice recordings you choose to save during speaking practice.

These stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you use speaking practice, the app records your voice and turns it into text with Apple's speech recognition. Apple may process the audio according to its own privacy policy. Recordings are not sent to us.

AI features

The AI conversation, answer checking, word coaching and dialogue generation send the Japanese text you type, or speak and have transcribed, to Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic, PBC, through our server (a proxy).

  • The app asks for your consent before anything is sent, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → AI features.
  • No name, account or device identifier is sent with the text.
  • Our proxy does not store the text. Anthropic processes it to produce the answer under its own terms.

Radio

The Radio tab under Listening reads public podcast feeds (NHK radio news, NHK Yasashii Kotoba News and podcasts for Japanese learners) and plays the audio directly from each publisher's servers. The app does not redistribute the audio. These requests carry no information identifying you; like any internet request, the publisher's server sees your IP address. Episodes you download for offline listening stay on your device and are deleted with the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: developer@ars-dev.co.jp
